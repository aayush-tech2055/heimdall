# Glimmora Brand Guidelines

*Version 1.0 · November 2026 · Fictional brand for testing Heimdall*

Glimmora is an oral-care brand sold across India. Our range is Glimmora Active Shield (everyday fluoride toothpaste), Glimmora Herbal Care (neem and clove), Glimmora Sensitive Relief and Glimmora Kids Strawberry. These guidelines apply to everything we publish: TV and video ad scripts, print ads, posters, social media posts, packaging copy, emails and influencer briefs. Every piece of content is checked against them before it is published.

## 1. Voice and tone

- Sound like a friendly, caring dental expert: calm, reassuring and confident.
- Never use fear, shame or disgust to sell. Do not mock anyone's breath, teeth, smile or hygiene.
- Address the reader directly as "you" and write in the active voice.
- Write in simple, everyday English and avoid dental jargon. If you must use a technical term such as "enamel erosion" or "gingivitis", explain it in plain words the first time.
- Use at most one exclamation mark per piece of content, and never write whole words in capital letters for emphasis.
- Our communication should always feel fresh and premium.

## 2. Terminology and product names

- Always write our name as Glimmora: one word, capital G. Never write "Glimora", "Glimmorra" or "Glimoora".
- Use the full product names exactly as written: Glimmora Active Shield, Glimmora Herbal Care, Glimmora Sensitive Relief and Glimmora Kids Strawberry. Never shorten them to nicknames such as "the shield paste" or "the sensitive one".
- Never use these words: "miracle", "cure", "guaranteed", "permanent", "painless".
- Call the people we talk to "you" or "families". Do not call them "consumers" or "users".
- Say "toothpaste", not "tooth cream" or "dentifrice".

## 3. Messaging and claims

- Lead with the everyday benefit (fresher breath, stronger enamel, comfort for sensitive teeth), not with a list of ingredients.
- Every number, percentage, price, ranking or clinical result must match the approved Glimmora fact sheet.
- Never claim that a product cures, reverses or permanently fixes any dental condition. Our toothpastes help prevent and protect; they do not treat disease.
- Do not use superlatives such as "best", "No. 1" or "India's most trusted" unless an approved fact supports them.
- Dentist-recommendation claims may only use the approved dentist survey figure, and must name the survey.
- Headlines should use capital letters for every word, for example "FRESH BREATH ALL DAY", so they stand out.
- Messaging should be powerful and memorable.

## 4. Visuals

- Use only Glimmora teal (#00897B) and pearl white (#F8FAFA) as main colours, with mint (#A7E8D0) as the only accent. Never use red or orange backgrounds.
- Place the Glimmora logo on teal or pearl white only. Never stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects to it.
- Whenever a toothbrush with paste is shown, show a pea-sized amount of toothpaste, never a full-length strip across the bristles.
- Show children under 6 brushing only with an adult present.
- Never show another brand's packaging, logo or product in our visuals.

## 5. Accessibility

- Text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background. Never put white text on the mint accent colour.
- Every image must have alt text that describes what the image shows.
- Every video must have captions that match what is said, and on-screen text must stay visible for at least 3 seconds.
- Keep sentences under 20 words so that everyone can follow them easily.
- Do not use "click here" or "tap here" as link or button text. Say where the link goes, for example "Shop Glimmora Active Shield".
- Use plain everyday English instead of dental jargon, and explain any technical term the first time it appears.

## 6. Legal and regulatory

- Never mention competitor brands by name or show their products, including Whitebay and Pearlo. Never make claims about another brand's toothpaste.
- Any content that mentions Glimmora Kids Strawberry must include the line "For children under 6, use a pea-sized amount under adult supervision."
- Never publish a customer's name, photo, phone number or email address without their written consent.
- Award and rating claims must name the awarding body and the year.
- Every advertisement must end with the line "Results may vary. Use twice daily as directed."
