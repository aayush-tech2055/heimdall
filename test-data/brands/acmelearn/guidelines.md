# AcmeLearn Brand Guidelines

*Version 3.2 · Fictional brand for the Heimdall test set*

AcmeLearn is an online learning platform that helps people across India build practical skills through short video courses, guided projects and live mentor sessions. These guidelines apply to everything we publish: blog posts, social media captions, ads, emails and video scripts.

## 1. Tone of voice

- Use a warm, encouraging tone. Never mock, shame or pressure learners about their skills, age, background or progress.
- Address the reader directly as "you" and write in the active voice.
- Content should feel exciting and on-brand.
- Use at most one exclamation mark per piece of content, and never write whole words in capital letters for emphasis.

## 2. Terminology

- Never use these words: "cheap", "hack", "dummies", "guaranteed job".
- Always write our name as AcmeLearn, one word. Never write "Acme Learn" or "Acme-Learn".
- Our paid plans are called AcmeLearn Plus and AcmeLearn Pro. Never call them "premium plan" or "gold plan".
- Call the people who use AcmeLearn "learners". Do not call them "students".

<!-- page -->

## 3. Messaging and claims

- Lead with what learners can do after a course (skills, projects, portfolio pieces), not with a list of platform features.
- Messaging should be impactful and inspire trust.
- Never promise jobs, salaries, placements or income. We teach skills; we do not guarantee careers.
- Every number, price, ranking or statistic must match the approved AcmeLearn fact sheet.
- Do not use superlatives such as "best", "No. 1" or "India's top" unless an approved fact supports them.
- Always state the monthly price of the plan you are promoting.
- Do not describe any course or plan as cheap.

<!-- page -->

## 4. Visuals and accessibility

- Use only the AcmeLearn indigo (#3B2F8F) and saffron (#F4A300) as accent colours, on white or very light grey backgrounds.
- Place the AcmeLearn logo on a white or indigo background only. Never stretch, rotate or recolour it.
- Every image must have alt text that describes what the image shows.
- Write so that a 14-year-old can follow: keep sentences under 25 words and explain any technical term the first time you use it.
- Use inclusive, gender-neutral language: write "everyone" or "folks", not "guys".

<!-- page -->

## 5. Legal

- Social media captions must not mention prices. Link to acmelearn.example/pricing instead.
- Never mention competitors by name, including Coursify and SkillNest.
- Do not use a learner's name, photo or results without their written consent, and never publish anyone's phone number or email address.
- Every piece of promotional content must end with the line "T&Cs apply."
- Award and rating claims must name the source and the year.
