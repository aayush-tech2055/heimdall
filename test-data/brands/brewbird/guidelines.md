# Brewbird Coffee Brand Guidelines

*Version 1.4 · Fictional brand for the Heimdall test set*

Brewbird Coffee roasts single-estate Indian coffee in small batches and delivers it fresh to homes and cafés. These guidelines apply to every product page, social post, email, ad and in-store sign.

## 1. Tone of voice

- Sound friendly and curious, like a barista chatting with a regular. Be playful, but never sarcastic or rude about other people's coffee choices.
- Keep it simple: use everyday words, not coffee-industry jargon, unless you explain it.
- Posts should have good energy and feel premium.
- Talk about coffee as an everyday pleasure. Never describe it as an addiction or a drug.

<!-- page -->

## 2. Terminology

- Never use these words: "instant", "cheap", "addictive", "jitter juice".
- Write our name as Brewbird, one word. Never write "Brew Bird" or "Brew-bird".
- Our subscription is called the Brewbird Flight Club. Never call it a "membership" or a "coffee box".
- Call the people who grow our coffee "estate partners". Do not call them "suppliers".

<!-- page -->

## 3. Claims

- Never make health claims. Do not say coffee boosts immunity, burns fat, cures headaches or prevents disease.
- Every price, rating, origin, roast date or statistic must match the approved Brewbird fact sheet.
- Do not say "best coffee in India" or "world's best" or any similar superlative unless an approved fact supports it.
- Always mention the estate a coffee comes from when you name a coffee.
- Origin claims must be specific and true; never call a blend "single-estate".
- Words like "great taste" should be used carefully.

<!-- page -->

## 4. Visuals and accessibility

- Use the Brewbird palette only: roast brown (#4A2C2A), crema (#F3E5D0) and leaf green (#2E7D4F).
- Product photos must show the real packaging; never show a competitor's cup or bag.
- Every image needs alt text that describes the product and the setting.
- Never put text over a busy photo; text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.
- Every video must have captions that match what is said.
- Never refer to coffee as a drug or an addiction.

<!-- page -->

## 5. Legal

- Every post that promotes a product must end with the line "Contains caffeine. Not recommended for children or pregnant women."
- Never mention competitors by name, including Bean Street and Kaapi Kart.
- Discounts must state the end date of the offer.
- Do not name our estate partners or their estates in public posts; partner names are confidential.
- Do not use customer reviews or photos without written permission.
- Never collect or publish customers' phone numbers or addresses in public posts.
